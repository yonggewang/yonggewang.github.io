Selected contribution to industrial and government standards by Yongge Wang

  • W3C and IETF
    • S.Blake-Wilson, G. Karlinger, T. Kobayashi, and Y.Wang: ECDSA with XML-Signature Syntax, IETF RFC 4050, 2000.
    • Draft: Internet Key Exchange (IPSec IKE) with MQV (pdf)
  • American National Standards (ANSI)
    • ANSI X9.92, Public Key Cryptography For The Financial Services Industry: PV-Digital Signature Scheme Giving Partial Message Recovery (pdf, 560K)
  • IEEE 1363
    • Password authentication protocol SRP5 (full 1363.2, only srp5)
    • Identity Based Key Agreement WANG-KE (full 1363.3)
  • IETF (IP Storage--iSCSI)
    • DH-CHAP was originally proposed for standardization in iSCSI working group. Due to my analysis and attacks, it was finally dropped from the standards. My post and follow-ups could be found here.
  • Japan Government IPA Project
    • Evaluated the five public key cryptography signature schemes submitted to Japanese government IPA CRYPTREC. The reports could be obtained from the official IPA project pages. For example, you can find ESIGN, ESIGN Identification Scheme, My-ELLTY Signature Scheme, and ACE Signature Scheme.
