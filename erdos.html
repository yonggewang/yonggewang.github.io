My Erdoes number

Yongge Wang's Erdoes-Number is Three Paul Erdoes is one of the most productive mathematicians having more than 500 coauthors. The Erdoes Number of a person A is computed as the length of the shortest path from Paul Erdoes to the person A with respect to the graph of co-authors of scientific articles. Yongge Wang's Erdoes number is 3 which is witnessed by the following paths and publications.
Paths
  • Yongge Wang <--> Yvo Desmedt <--> Andrew Odlyzko <--> Paul Erdoes
  • Yongge Wang <--> Cristian Calude <--> Solomon Marcus <--> Paul Erdoes
